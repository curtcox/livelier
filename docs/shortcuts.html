Keyboard and touch

ActionKeyboard or gesture
Run main scriptCtrl/Cmd+Enter
Open haloAlt/Option-click, Ctrl/Cmd-click, or 500 ms long-press
World or morph menuRight-click; long-press the world background
Close halo/menuEscape
Undo direct editCtrl/Cmd+Z
Redo direct editShift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z
Workspace do itCtrl/Cmd+D
Workspace print itCtrl/Cmd+P
Workspace inspect itCtrl/Cmd+I
Move a morphDrag a draggable morph or its Move halo handle
ReparentDrag a grabbable morph or the Grab halo handle into a drop container
Zoom worldPinch with two fingers, 0.25×–4×
Pan worldDrag with two fingers
Reset viewFit
Resize editorDrag the splitter; focus it and use Left/Right

On phones, use the World, Code, and Console tabs. Halo mode makes a single tap select a halo. Tap editable Text to bring up the keyboard. Controls and inputs use a minimum 40 px touch target on the phone layout.
